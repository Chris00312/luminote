Welcome

Your internet memory library.

Mark what matters on any page. Build a searchable knowledge base. Let AI read and write alongside you.

Feature 1 — Mark

Highlight any text. Get instant clarity.

Select text on any webpage or PDF, click Mark, and LumiNote explains it — brief, detailed, or deep — with related concepts you can click to explore further.

Feature 2 — Visual AI

Don't just read — capture what you see.

Screenshot any chart, diagram, or photo. LumiNote describes it, extracts the key entities, and saves it alongside your text notes.

Feature 3 — Moodboard

Arrange memories on an infinite canvas.

Group related marks visually. Drag, resize, and export the whole board as one PNG to share the thinking behind a decision.

Feature 4 — Agent

An AI that reasons about your moodboard.

Ask questions. The agent can read every card, add new ones, and update notes — with every tool call shown inline, so you always see what it did.
